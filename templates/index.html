Key definitions
  • Relevant main subject is the environment, climate or sustainability (emissions, energy transition, pollution, waste, nature, environmental impact of business or policy).
  • Not relevant everything else, incl. social topics, governance and pure market news.
  • Weather: a storm, flood or fire is Relevant only if linked to an environmental cause, impact, pollution or policy.
  • Watch out: “political climate”, “Miami Heat”, “clean sheet” are Not relevant.
